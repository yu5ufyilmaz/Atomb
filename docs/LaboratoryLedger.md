### Sağ sayfa ayracı (26 Ekim düzenlemesi)

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: transparent game UI sprite component for the RIGHT page edge of an existing laboratory notebook.
Input image is the EXACT existing notebook whose body must be kept unchanged in the game. It is a material, lighting and paper reference. Produce ONLY ONE small standalone right-side subject-divider paper tab accessory, NOT a new notebook, NOT the notebook body. It will be layered under the existing page.
Primary request: a die-cut built-in subject index tab belonging to a divider SHEET in a multi-subject notebook. It must look like an integral extension of a thin warm grey-ivory paper divider, not an attached sticker, button or bookmark.
Composition: perfectly flat front-on orthographic cutout, tall portrait shape. The LEFT half is a straight vertical paper root/fore-edge strip, about twice the height of the visible ear, intended to be hidden beneath the original notebook. From the middle of that root a narrow vertical rounded rectangular EAR protrudes to the RIGHT. Concave curved shoulder transitions smoothly flow from sheet edge into the top and bottom of the ear; there is no separating seam between sheet and ear. Visible ear proportions approx 1 wide by 2.5 tall, with enough blank smooth space for vertically rotated runtime text. Root and ear are one single continuous die-cut sheet.
Materials: match the attached notebook's muted warm grey ivory paper, restrained fine paper grain, tiny layered sheet edges on the right/bottom, a subtly reinforced translucent edge, very gentle contact shading on the lower edge. Barely visible thickness; clean functional institutional stationery. Moderate stylization matching the original, not hyperreal, not parchment.
Color palette: grey ivory, very faint desaturated sage tint. Soft neutral even light, no harsh cast shadow. Outer silhouette softly squared rounded corners, not pill-shaped. Fill most of the canvas, small transparent margin.
Constraints: transparent background, one connected component only, no text, no letters, no numbers, no black printed outline, no rectangular UI frame, no selection marker, no metal, no cloth, no spiral, no adhesive sticker, no detached layers, no notebook body, no mockup, no scenery. The flat left root joins the right ear seamlessly. Preserve alpha.
